# Step 151: Compound Intent Triage Clarification - Decisions

### Decision 1: Compound Inquiry Handling Policy
- **Choice:** Triage Clarification (ask customer to prioritize which inquiry to resolve first).
- **Reasoning:** In complex customer emails containing conflicting or multi-departmental requests (e.g. troubleshooting a hardware problem while also demanding billing cancellation), attempting to answer both simultaneously creates customer confusion, fragments troubleshooting focus, and risks commercial misalignment. Prompting the customer with an automated clarification to select their primary priority ensures one issue is handled end-to-end cleanly before moving to the next.
