# Decision: Refine Operations and Knowledge Navigation Hierarchy

## Rationale
- `AI Pipeline Trace` represents the operational step-by-step telemetry of how incoming emails are parsed and answered by the AI agent in real time, making it an operational diagnostic view alongside Mail Monitor, Drafts, and Tickets.
- Renaming `AI & Knowledge` to `Knowledge` creates a crisp, unambiguous label dedicated to static organizational intelligence and the retrieval corpus (`Knowledge Base (RAG)`).
