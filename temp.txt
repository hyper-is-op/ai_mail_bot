**Smarter validation on AI-drafted CRM connections** — currently, an AI-drafted connection is checked for the right shape but not test-run against a real sample response before you see it. The plan is to actually validate it against your sample data before showing you the draft, catching a bad configuration before it even reaches the approval stage rather than after.
- **Stronger data separation guarantees** — an ongoing review of exactly how sample data you provide when setting up a CRM connection flows through the system, to make sure it can never end up mixed into another client's context.
